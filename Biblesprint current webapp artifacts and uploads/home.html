Hi, I’m Timi. I was inspired to build Bible Sprint after being taught by the Lord how to read the whole Bible in 43 days. Since then many others have joined me in a continuous cycle of reading the Word of God in large volumes, every day. The secret is reading in sprints of 7 chapters straight, then repeat. Reading the Bible in large chunks gives clarity and helps you understand the scriptures in more wholistic ways. It empowers you in many ways and activates a deeper spiritual walk with God. Join me.

How it works

3 simple steps. No guesswork. You just read.

1

Choose your pace

Gentle (1 sprint a day), Steady (2), Brisk (3), or Sprint (4). Finish the whole Bible in 43 days — or up to 170 if you want a gentler rhythm.

2

Follow the sprints

Each sprint is seven chapters. Tap through a single sprint in a focused sitting. No decisions, no scrolling, no picking — just read and tick.

3

Complete the Bible

Journal key verses, observations, applications, and prayers as you go. Finish the Bible cover-to-cover. Start another round.

What makes this different

Most Bible apps give you tools and leave you to figure out the schedule. Bible Sprint is a structured, finish-able programme.

  • Structured — no guessing what to read
  • Built to finish — every chapter, no gaps, no repeats
  • Built-in audio for every chapter
  • Pick your pace — gentle to sprint, 43 days or more
  • Pick your translation — NIV, ESV, KJV, NLT, and more
  • Start in Genesis or in Matthew
  • Read with friends — Sprint Circles for accountability
  • Choose your colour theme — gold, coral, or deep blue, in Settings
  • Journal as you read — your notes stay private
  • Share with friends
